Pre-change dependency audit (2026-10-14)

Canonical hierarchy candidates:
organisation.ReportingRelationship: employee OneToOne -> EmployeeProfile;
manager FK -> EmployeeProfile; company FK; no_self_manager check; implicit FK
indexes and unique employee index. No other model points to this table.
Consumers: organisation services, serializers, views, growth visibility;
review launch; task visibility/team scope; reports; demo seed; promotion guard;
foundation, growth and reporting-display tests. Frontend: people dropdown,
recursive hierarchy, work/growth team filters, employee detail.
Decision: remove after validation/normalization. Manager identity remains a
derived read-only API value; no mapping table needed for two levels.

companies.Membership.is_manager: contexts, login filter, platform role display,
employee create/update, task/report permission checks, access approvals, preview
and demo commands, tests. No independent business capability remains: all such
responsibilities move to active Head of HR. Remove column and assignment role.
Keep manager workspace context as a capability of Head of HR, not a role flag.

Keep Membership.is_hr: separate HR administration permission, not a reporting
relationship. Keep is_head_hr/active/company_id and unique company/user.
Keep Review.manager_id and reviewer_id: historical appointed participants;
Submission.author_id/kind, FinalSnapshot, ReviewEvent and AuditLog immutable
records must survive. Pending responsibilities resolve dynamically to current
Head of HR; completed records retain original manager identity.
Keep WorkItem parent_task_id/assigned_by_id/accountable_owner_id/assigned_to_id:
historical work provenance/delegation, not employee reporting relationships.
Keep Commitment.owner_id and manager_support: historical agreed plans.
Keep GrowthEntry.owner_id/subject_id/shared: authorship, subject and consent.
Keep job roles, senior_leader and departments: evaluation/organisation metadata,
not hierarchy levels. Keep invitation/access-request details and event JSON.

No reports_to field, parent_manager field, hierarchy depth column, membership
manager join table or separate tree metadata was found. Old initial migrations
must retain historical schema definitions; new migrations perform the cleanup.
Celery review reminders use stored review participants and must use effective
manager for unfinished reviews. No hierarchy table references in audit FKs.

Safety boundary: validate every existing company has exactly one active Head
of HR with a same-company profile before any destructive operation. Missing or
duplicate heads fail with company IDs; never infer from designation/is_manager.
Normalize links before drop. Preserve all employee/review/evidence/growth/audit/
company/invitation/access-request rows. Production application is a separate
deployment step; do not apply destructive migrations during local development.

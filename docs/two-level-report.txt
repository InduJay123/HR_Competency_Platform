2. Implemented model
   Active company Head of HR is resolved centrally by company; Head has no
   effective manager and all other employees resolve to that Head. My Team is
   all other active company employees. Ordinary employees have no team/manager
   capability. Arbitrary reporting fields return 400; old /reporting/ endpoint
   is removed (404). Manager login/approval role and assignment controls removed.
   Existing manager workspace URLs remain as Head of HR capabilities.
   Missing/multiple Heads fail closed. Company approval/bootstrap creates its
   Head transactionally; employee creation requires a valid Head. Last-Head
   deactivation/demotion is blocked in ordinary writes and promotion commands.
   DB enforces at most one active Head with a partial unique constraint;
   at-least-one is enforced through supported creation/transfer/business paths
   and migration preflight (not a cross-table CHECK on arbitrary direct SQL).

8. Data migration behavior
   Lock company rows; validate ALL companies before writes. Fail with IDs for
   missing/duplicate Heads, missing/invalid Head profiles, tenant mismatches or
   unfinished Head self-reviews. Never infer assignments from title or old flag.
   PostgreSQL unexpected incoming FKs/dependent views (including flag views)
   block cleanup rather than being silently removed by CASCADE.
   Remove Head's old reporting link; normalize active employees to Head; then
   drop the redundant table atomically. Flush deferred PostgreSQL FK checks
   before the DROP. Add partial unique constraint and remove manager flag.
   No existing review, submission, snapshot or audit is rewritten by migrations.
   Unfinished review responsibility derives from current Head. Sealed forms
   remain sealed; takeover of an old draft archives previous content/authorship
   in a new ReviewEvent. New finalization records final responsible participants
   and retains original appointments in its snapshot. Already completed records
   stay byte-for-byte unchanged. Growth shared records follow current Head.
   Controlled repair: set_head_hr requires explicit company/membership/active
   platform-admin IDs and a reason, validates tenant/profile, records an event,
   and refuses a candidate with an unfinished self-review. Raw parameterized
   SQL is limited to this command's old/new-schema-compatible role update and
   schema preflight; migrations use Django operations for schema removal.
